Diffusion Language Models

The explainer From typewriter to editor: diffusion models and diffusion LLMs, grounded in the nine papers on Inception Labs' about page. Interactive simulations, Plotly figures, faded derivations, a 25-question quiz, and a concept map. The review deck Spaced repetition over the explainer's questions (Leitner boxes, 1→30 days). Progress is stored in your browser — works great on a phone. The cheat sheet Every formula with its one-sentence punchline, on one printable page. Anki deck (.apkg) The same 25 questions for Anki users — download and import. The lab: build it yourself Implement masked diffusion in numpy against a pytest suite that knows the right answers.
